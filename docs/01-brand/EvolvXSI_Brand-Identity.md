## Brand promise

**Working promise:** help organizations turn their knowledge, tools, and policies into agent workflows that people can review, approve, and improve.

**One-line description:** EvolvXSI is a proposed platform for building and operating governed AI agent workflows, with specialized solutions for specific industries and business functions.

## Positioning statement

For teams responsible for repeatable, evidence-heavy work, EvolvXSI provides a shared way to run AI-assisted workflows with source visibility, human control, and measurable quality. The first market and buyer remain to be validated.

## Voice and terminology

- Use plain, specific language and describe the work completed by an agent.
- Say “AI-assisted” when a human remains responsible for review or approval.
- Distinguish a **task** (one request), **workflow** (versioned sequence), **run** (one execution), and **vertical solution** (domain package).
- Use extension terms consistently: a **connector** links a data source or app; a **skill** is a versioned reusable capability; a **plugin** is a signed package that adds skills, tools or interface; an **MCP server** is a tool server reached through the Model Context Protocol; a **pack** bundles agents, skills, templates and rules for a domain.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as “superhuman,” “fully autonomous,” “error-free,” or guaranteed compliance. “Super intelligent” may remain in vision language but must not imply proven capability.

## Visual direction to test

The visual identity is open. Test a calm, technical interface that makes task status, evidence, review points, and responsibility clear. Before selecting colors, typography, or a logo, assess contrast, accessibility, enterprise credibility, and whether the name is legally usable.

## Approval checklist

Complete domain and legal checks; choose the launch audience; test the one-line description with prospective users; approve brand tokens and a public claim review process. Until then, this is an internal working identity.
